    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Are my images uploaded anywhere?

    No. The PDF engine ships inside this page as a local script. Every decode, resize and encode happens in your browser tab using canvas. Close the tab and nothing remains.

    Which image formats are supported?

    Anything your browser can decode — JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF and SVG are guaranteed. HEIC and multi-page TIFF work only if your browser exposes a decoder for them, which some desktop and Safari builds do.

    Does it reduce image quality?

    Only as much as you ask. Original keeps every pixel at the maximum JPEG quality setting you chose. Lower DPI caps and lower quality percentages are always opt-in, and images are never upscaled.

    How big a PDF can it handle?

    Dozens of pages is comfortable on a laptop. If you are combining hundreds of full-resolution photos, select 150 DPI in Resolution — that alone usually cuts the file by 80%.

    Is the password protection real?

    Yes. The PDF is encrypted with the standard security handler, so a compliant reader will genuinely ask for the password before opening it. Permissions such as printing and copying are enforced by the reader, not by this page.

    Can I override settings for a single page?

    Yes. Each card has its own paper size, orientation and fit selectors. A page that differs from the document default is tagged with a dot, and Use document default puts it back in line.
